FAQ

Questions, answered

Who pays when I invite my team?

Only the workspace owner. Limits follow the owner's plan, so one Pro subscription covers everyone in the workspace. Teammates can keep a free workspace of their own as well.

What happens when I hit a Free limit?

Nothing is lost. You can keep working with everything you have; Orbit just asks you to upgrade before adding contact 251 or deal 51, or inviting someone.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 14-day Pro trial starts without one. If you don't subscribe, your workspace returns to the Free limits and keeps all its data.

Can I leave a workspace I was invited to?

Yes, from Settings → Members. The records you own stay with the workspace, and you keep your own workspace.

How do I sign in?

With your Yard account. Orbit never sees or stores a password.
